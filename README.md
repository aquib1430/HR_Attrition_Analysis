# HR Attrition Dashboard – Tableau Project

## 📊 Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze HR data and monitor employee hiring, termination, demographics, income distribution, and departmental performance. The dashboard helps HR teams identify attrition patterns and make data-driven workforce decisions.

## 🧾 Objective
- Track and compare the number of employees hired vs. terminated.
- Analyze attrition trends across departments, locations, and demographics.
- Study the relationship between education, performance, salary, and retention.
- Provide actionable insights to improve employee retention strategies.

## 🔧 Tools Used
- Tableau
- Excel

## 📌 Key Metrics
- **Total Active Employees:** 7,984
- **Employees Hired:** 8,950
- **Employees Terminated:** 966
- **Attrition Rate:** ~10.8%
- Department-wise hiring and termination counts.
- Gender distribution (Male: 54%, Female: 46%).

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations
- Employee hiring vs. termination trend lines
- Attrition by department (Operations, Sales, IT, Finance, etc.)
- Employee distribution by location (HQ vs. Branch)
- Demographic analysis (Gender, Age, Education)
- Salary analysis by Education & Gender
- Age vs. Salary scatter plot by Job Title

## 🧠 Insights
- Operations has the highest number of both hires and terminations.
- Most employees hold Bachelor’s degrees, followed by Master’s.
- Employees with higher education tend to have higher salaries.
- Majority of workforce is aged 35–44, which also corresponds to higher salaries.
- HQ employs 70% of total staff compared to 30% at branch offices.

## ✅ Recommendations
- Investigate high attrition rates in Operations and Sales to identify underlying issues.
- Focus retention strategies on mid-level employees aged 35–44 with high performance.
- Encourage career development programs for employees with Bachelor’s degrees to improve retention.
- Improve performance management in departments with low “Excellent” ratings.
